FAQ

Questions, answered

What does LaunchIO create?

From a short description of your idea, LaunchIO generates a pitch deck, a logo, a financial overview and a business plan. Everything you generate is saved in your archive inside the app.

Do I need to create an account?

No. LaunchIO creates an anonymous account for you automatically the first time you open the app. Your coins, purchases and history are linked to it. Because it isn’t tied to an email address, deleting the app or changing devices may cause it to be lost.

How do coins work?

Generating materials uses coins. Coins are sold as one-time packs through in-app purchase — there is no subscription and nothing renews automatically. Coins have no cash value and can’t be transferred.

Can I rely on the numbers and plans?

Treat them as a starting point. AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete, and it is not business, legal, financial or investment advice. Review everything before you rely on it or share it. See our Terms of Use.

Is my idea used to train AI?

We don’t use your content to train our own AI models. What you submit is sent to our AI service provider to create the materials you ask for. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Who owns what I generate?

You keep your rights in what you submit and, to the extent the law allows, you own the output generated for you. Make sure your use of it doesn’t infringe anyone else’s rights, such as trademarks. The details are in our Terms of Use.

How do I delete my data?

Email launchio.ai@gmail.com and we’ll delete your data within 30 days. Because we don’t know your name or email address, include details that help us find your records, such as the approximate date of a recent generation or an App Store order ID.
